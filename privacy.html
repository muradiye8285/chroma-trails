Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chroma Trails (“the App”), developed by Muradiye Kayaduman (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the App.

1. Information stored on your device

Chroma Trails stores gameplay information locally on your device so the App can remember your progress. This may include completed levels, stars, selected difficulty, Daily Puzzle streak, Master Mode progress, onboarding status, and App Store review-prompt status.

This gameplay information is not uploaded to our servers. The App does not require an account, and we do not operate a database containing your gameplay progress.

2. Advertising and consent

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. A rewarded advertisement is shown only when you choose to watch one for a gameplay hint.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and other device or usage information. The precise information processed depends on your region, device settings, consent choices, and whether personalized or non-personalized advertising is permitted.

Where required, the App displays Google’s consent form before requesting advertisements. If privacy options are required in your region, you can review or change your advertising choices from the App’s Settings screen.

For more information, review Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Information we do not request

The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health information, or payment-card details. Chroma Trails does not include user accounts, social messaging, or user-generated content uploads.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google for advertising, fraud prevention, security, measurement, and service operation as described in Google’s policies and according to your consent choices. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect legal rights, safety, and security.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay progress remains on your device until you reset progress in the App or delete the App. The Reset Progress option removes campaign results, stars, Daily Puzzle progress, and Master Mode progress stored by the App. Deleting the App removes its locally stored data from the device, subject to standard iOS backup behavior.

Advertising information processed by Google is retained according to Google’s own retention policies. Requests concerning data processed by Google should be directed to Google through its privacy tools and policies.

6. Children’s privacy

Chroma Trails is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and consent behavior may vary based on region, age-related settings, and applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review the request.

7. International processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their processing is governed by their privacy policies and applicable safeguards.

8. Your choices

  • Use the advertising privacy option in the App’s Settings screen when it is available.
  • Manage device-level privacy and advertising settings in iOS Settings.
  • Reset locally stored gameplay progress from the App’s Settings screen.
  • Delete the App to remove its locally stored information.
  • Contact us with a privacy question or request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The latest version will be published on this page, with the updated date shown above.
