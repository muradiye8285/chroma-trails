Frequently asked questions

Quick solutions

How do I play?

Drag from one colored dot to the matching dot. Trails cannot cross each other or pass through blocked cells. Connect every pair to complete the level.

Why did my rewarded hint not appear?

An internet connection and an available advertisement are required. Wait a moment and try again. You receive a hint only after the rewarded advertisement finishes successfully.

Can I change advertising privacy choices?

When required in your region, open Settings inside Chroma Trails and select Ad Privacy to review the available consent options.

How do I reset the game?

Open Settings and choose Reset Progress. After confirmation, campaign progress, stars, Daily Puzzle streak, and Master Mode progress are removed from the device.

Is progress synchronized between devices?

No. Game progress is stored locally on the device. Chroma Trails currently does not use an account or cloud synchronization.

What should I include in a support email?

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, App version, selected difficulty, level number, and a short description of what happened.
